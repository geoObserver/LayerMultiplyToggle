about=(26.05.2026) Adds a toolbar split-button that toggles a layer blend mode for all layers or only the selected layers/groups. The blend mode (Multiply, Screen, Overlay, Darken, Lighten) is chosen from a dropdown, and the same actions are available from the layer-tree right-click menu (QGIS 3.32+). Original blend modes are preserved and restored on toggling off, and the on/off state plus chosen mode are stored per project. Compatible with QGIS 3.x (Qt5) and QGIS 4.x (Qt6).
